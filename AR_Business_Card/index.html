AR / VR Mini Project

Your card,
come alive.

  1. 1Hold the printed card (or the image on your phone) up to the webcam.
  2. 2Point at a button with your index finger.
  3. 3Pinch thumb and finger, or just hold still for a moment, to press.
  4. 4Press ZOOM, then spread or pinch your fingers to resize. Scrolling works too.

The mouse works too. Press M to mirror, Z to zoom, H for stats.
